Hi! I'm Ryan Yin, a rising sophomore at the University of Illinois. I'm mainly interested in computer and network security, but I'm interested in a lot of different areas of computer science, from theory to machine learning to human-computer interaction and beyond! 

At the University of Illinois, I'm a Admin and Embedded Lead in [SIGPwny](https://sigpwny.com), a student organization dedicated to security and privacy. I've presented on topics such as [SSRF](https://sigpwny.com/meetings/general/2026-01-29/) and [cryptography](https://sigpwny.com/meetings/general/2025-10-16/). As an Embedded Lead, I lead a subteam of Embedded Team (SIGPwny's team for embedded system security) members on an aspect of our Embedded Capture the Flag (eCTF) performance, ranging from defense to attack. In 2026, I led the compiler security subteam, developing several security mitigations with LLVM, including a shadow stack and stack zeroization. I also designed a system to punish voltage glitching. We placed 4th worldwide this year!

I'm also an Exec Board Intern of the [Hong Kong Student Association](https://linktr.ee/uiuchksa) at the University of Illinois. I'm joining the Event Committee (and possibly Fundraising) next year!

Outside of school, I enjoy playing badminton, chess, and reading.
